Open Submissions

Vespertine Literary is now accepting these works for our debut issue All That Slumbers:

Poetry, prose, short stories, creative nonfiction, essays, articles, photography, visual art.


General Submission Guidelines

  1. All submitted pieces should be your own work and previously unpublished. In the case of public domain images that may pair with your work, please provide a proper accreditation.
  2. All submitted pieces must align with the issue’s theme.
  3. Please do not submit to Vespertine Literary if you use generative AI at any point in your creative process.
  4. You may only submit the form once, but are free to include multiple pieces in that submission (ex: 1 visual artwork, 2 written works, and one poetry collection can all be uploaded under one submission).

Specific Submission Guidelines

Poetry or Poetry Collection:

  1. Submit 1-5 unpublished poems in one PDF/document
  2. The document should have one poem per page

Short Stories:

  1. Submit 1-3 unpublished short stories in multiple PDF documents.
  2. Maximum word count across all written works should be 6,000.

Other Written Works:

  1. Submit as many unpublished works as you’d like.
  2. Maximum word count across all written works should be 6,000.

Original Visual Art:

  1. Submit 1-3 unpublished art pieces in PNG or JPEG format.
  2. All artwork should be original and not AI-generated.
