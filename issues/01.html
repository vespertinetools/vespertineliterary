All That Slumbers

The debut issue of Vespertine Literary, All That Slumbers, invites artists to investigate forces that creatively “haunt” their works: memories from times long past, places that no longer exist, and all that lies beneath the dirt but is never truly gone. Framed by the stark monochrome of winter and the gothic lens that informs Vespertine Literary at its core, All That Slumbers provides creatives a place to unearth narrative ghosts in poems, artworks, and written works.
